seanmh.com — Project Plan

Canonical project status and roadmap. Consolidated and verified July 25, 2026.

North star: make the branch-per-version architecture the experience itself. Three portfolio interpretations share one domain and cinematic switching; the Nexus is a deliberately discovered 3D portal world; future experiments make the multiverse feel alive without weakening the portfolio's clarity, accessibility, or speed.

Architecture and operating decisions

AreaDecision to preserve
RoutingVERSIONS contains only a-scroll, b-card, and c-terminal. Nexus belongs in the explicit ROUTABLE set and must never be randomly assigned.
Shared UIcontent is canonical for VersionSwitcher.astro and transitions.css; those files remain byte-identical across the three portfolio branches.
Contenta-scroll intentionally diverges in parts of its content schema and imagery. Port relevant changes manually instead of blindly cherry-picking.
Progressive enhancementNavigation and content must survive unsupported View Transitions, reduced motion, disabled JavaScript, and unavailable WebGL.
Generated versionsAutomation may propose designs, but publishing stays behind a reviewable PR and explicit human approval.
ToolchainUse Node 22 or newer. Keep Astro, Tailwind, GSAP/Lenis, and Three.js responsibilities isolated to the branches that need them.
